# Phase 1 experiment protocol

Protocol version 1 was defined before implementation and before access to real evaluation data. Study D2R Catacombs Level 2 on Normal after stairs arrival from Level 1. Analyze NEXT to Level 3 and the floor's WP independently. Synthetic runs exercise the contract and are marked as such.

## Inputs and model

- A dataset has one build, game mode, automap presentation and capture setting. Use independent layouts and one representative per known layout group. Deduplicate translated, rotated and reflected copies of the annotated complete room-and-link graph before splitting. The validator rejects possible duplicates, including cross-split duplicates. Review near duplicates manually. Freeze the manifest before analysis. Report dataset ID and a hash of each input file.
- Use manually registered observations. The entrance defines the origin of an **automap-plane** coordinate system with positive X right and positive Y down. Bearings are in this plane. Freeze early feature labels before revealing later frames to the early annotator. The evaluator turns development labels into sectors from each frame's player position. The predictor receives only observations and directional counts. All target coordinates and IDs stay outside it.
- The baseline uses a distinct sector frequency table for NEXT and WP, keyed on checkpoint and the observable entrance-facing direction or two-way axis. At each orientation it tries the observed player's region (the origin or a reliable sector from the entrance) and then pools regions if the bucket is sparse. It backs off from a specific facing to its axis and then to a prior pooled across orientations. The Phase 0.5 source review supports testing an orientation cue. A verified D2R bearing rule for either still-hidden target after stairs arrival is unavailable. [Audit on map generation](audit/PHASE_0_AUDIT.md#map-generation-and-observation-audit)
- The geometry method adds a compact signature to the same orientation key. Its features are visible room count (capped at four), occupied room-centre sectors relative to the entrance, confirmed link count (capped at three) and branching degree (three or more), undirected corridor axes, visible unexplored departure directions, and known dead-end count (capped at two). Run baseline-plus-one-feature comparisons to screen individual cues. Each method falls back to the same entrance prior when its geometry bucket contains fewer than two independent development samples. With an unknown entrance direction, use the geometry bucket pooled across entrances.
- Each sampled layout contributes one vote at its checkpoint. Use only development samples whose target was judged hidden at that checkpoint. Keep missing labels or bearings that cannot be resolved in the denominator. Emit a sector when it has at least two votes, sole highest support, and at least two thirds of all votes. Keep conflicting supported sectors visible in the output. The reported share describes the sample. It carries no probability calibration.
- Supported states are **PREDICTED**, **AMBIGUOUS** for conflicting directions below the decision threshold, **INSUFFICIENT_DATA** for unreadable player position or absent visible geometry in a geometry method, and **UNKNOWN** for unsupported or unresolved evidence. Predictions carry a sector and vote counts when available. Known true coordinates stay in the evaluator.

## Scoring and controls

- Record stages 0 through 3 at arrival and after each of three subsequent observation attempts. Report actual visible room and connection counts and newly observed rooms and links. Geography can stay the same or change by multiple rooms at a checkpoint. Find the first checkpoint with at least three visible rooms. Use it for the primary comparison when exactly three rooms are visible and otherwise record an overshoot. Report all four checkpoints and repeat their comparison on the fixed cohort that stays eligible at all four.
- Calculate angles clockwise from map-up using `atan2(dx, -dy)`. The eight sectors N, NE, E, SE, S, SW, W, NW have centres at multiples of 45°. An exact boundary maps to the clockwise sector. The zero vector has no bearing. If stated point uncertainty can cross a sector boundary or cover the origin, the bearing is unresolved. Compute angular error against the predicted sector centre with wraparound.
- A target is eligible only if it is independently verified hidden through that checkpoint and its true bearing can be assigned. Count missing truth, missing player position, uncertain visibility, uncertain bearing, absent checkpoints and discovery. A target confirmed discovered stays out of later predictive scoring. An otherwise eligible empty geometry observation counts toward geometry coverage.
- Report accuracy among predictions, coverage, incorrect predictions per eligible case and per prediction, abstention, correct predictions per eligible case, sector-centre angular error, and stage-specific statuses. For repeated observable inputs, count disagreements in annotated target position and in sector within a stage. Report standalone accuracy with coverage and paired lift on mutually predicted layouts, with common coverage and a group-level 95% bootstrap interval when at least two independent pairs exist. A one-layout group contributes at most one score to any one stage or primary comparison.
- Keep the evaluation split untouched during rule selection. The development replay may use leave-one-layout-out evaluation with the same rules. For a negative control, shuffle resolved development target sectors separately by target, entrance orientation, player region and checkpoint. Keep the split and observation features fixed. Treat the result as a diagnostic for the evaluator and inspect sparsity and selection effects.
- Report cold loading and fitting, warm p50/p95/max for both main methods, and peak process working set where supported. Time an offline replay on a fresh process when recording results. The experiment has no calibrated confidence model, so reliability for probabilities remains unevaluated.

## Provisional decision

Use around 10–15 real independent layouts to check annotation quality and estimate pilot lift. For a subsequent final test, document the sample and precision plan using development evidence before opening evaluation labels. Per target, propose at least 70% accuracy and at least 60% shared prediction coverage at the primary comparison, at least 10 percentage points of paired lift over baseline with a 95% interval above zero, improved correct-per-eligible results on the fixed cohort after exploration, and warm p95 below 100 ms on the test machine. Pilot samples, unresolved annotation disagreement and wide intervals support **INCONCLUSIVE**. A useful baseline with a precisely small geometry lift can justify a **PIVOT** for that target. Synthetic results alone leave both real-world decisions inconclusive.
