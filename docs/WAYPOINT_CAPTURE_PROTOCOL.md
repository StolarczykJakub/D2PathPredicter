# Waypoint capture protocol

Give this procedure only to the owner or ground-truth reviewer. Keep the footage and marks private. Prefer new offline gameplay on the intended difficulty and entry scenario. Record build, mode, layout identity, resolution, zoom and game UI settings. Use one uninterrupted native-resolution recording with the automap visible and fixed in zoom and display mode.

1. Begin before the waypoint area is revealed. Leave the automap on as you approach and record the whole approach. Preserve the last clear view where the location is hidden and the first clear view showing observable waypoint evidence. If the relevant region goes behind the HUD or out of frame, bracket visibility with the adjacent clear frames and record the uncertainty.
2. Pause near the platform and hover to show its waypoint name while the automap and player marker stay visible. Record a short stationary view, then move onto the centre of the labeled platform where practical and hold again. Use normal interaction to confirm the waypoint if gameplay permits. Capture the player centred on the actual platform from a second approach, with the labeled scene or continuous movement tying the two views together. A persistent nearby colored glyph can corroborate this evidence when its physical association is verified.
3. In each clear view, mark the player automap point on the platform and at least three shared wall or opening landmarks. Repeat the fixed-zoom registration screen in the existing pilot protocol. Compare the on-platform marks transferred into one reference plane and document player marking, platform-placement and transfer uncertainties separately. Scene coordinates, tooltip position and cursor pixels are not automap points. A pass requires repeated aligned marks that agree within their documented uncertainty.
4. Record VERIFIED with layout, identity evidence, reference point and uncertainty, registration evidence, and a discovery interval. Retain UNCERTAIN if any of those warrant an unresolved result. A different layout can validate the capture procedure or provide its own WP label. It cannot establish the waypoint coordinates of `mapa-07`. Keep WP availability independent of NEXT.
