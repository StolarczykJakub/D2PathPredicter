# Phase 1.6.4 bilateral partial constellation protocol

Freeze one experimental configuration before executing comparisons. Use independently authored synthetic controls, the frozen mapa-07 development RAW views and the six validated mapa-06 calibration RAW views. The Phase 1.6.3 locked holdout failure motivates the experiment. The diagnostic has no holdout input. Use the original 6 px strict constellation and 4 px player-relative baseline as comparators. Keep Phase 1.6.3 scoring and eligibility intact.

## Matching rule

Use only observed native-axis, fixed-zoom marker centres, hue groups and marking radii. A compatible point assignment has equal hue or an unknown hue. Two assignments can coexist if they use distinct points on both sides and their signed directed vectors differ by at most the lesser of 6 px and the sum of the four glyph radii. Each assigned point pair proposes a translation. Checking the vectors is equivalent to checking every pair of translation proposals against that bound. The mean translation and its maximum point residual are descriptive. The mean lies within 6 px of every proposal by convexity. Vector uncertainty is the same pairwise model as the strict matcher and supplies no joint measurement-noise probability.

Enumerate all inclusion-maximal one-to-one assignments under these constraints. A maximal assignment represents its subassignments with the same point identities. Count, spatial span and reliable-triangle support are monotonic under extension. Distinct maximal assignments remain separate even at identical translation or residual. There is no ranking cutoff, coordinate clustering or score tie-break.

- **Compatible geometry** requires at least three pairs and at least two-thirds of the visible markers on each side, rounded up; this allows controlled marker loss while requiring a clear majority in both views.
- **Resolved 2D support** additionally requires at least 25% of each visible marker bounding-box area and a corresponding triangle with a longest edge of at least 24 px and shortest altitude greater than the sum of its three marker radii in each view. The 24 px baseline is four times the vector cap. The area floor guards against a small peripheral fit and the triangle test guards against collinearity. A zero-area cloud cannot resolve in 2D. These are diagnostic guards; earlier calibration retained 9/11 and 9/9 markers on moved views with at least 77.70% box-area coverage.
- A sole correspondence with 2D support resolves the alignment to one reference. Multiple assignments to a single reference indicate correspondence ambiguity. Multiple compatible references indicate reference ambiguity. Weak compatible assignments are retained and can contribute to ambiguity. A complete search with at least three markers per input and zero compatible candidates yields no-match. Unavailable inputs, insufficient marker counts, weak sole candidates and budget stops abstain. A no-match also abstains from identification. Unmatched or cropped points are unknown.
- Evaluator-only region labels group the two reviews of a region for historical comparability. An unambiguous corpus region requires one compatible labeled region, one correspondence per compatible reference and at least one resolved 2D fit. A corpus answer is distinct from a verified physical identity. Report the geometry-only reference outcome alongside the labeled evaluation. For baseline A, report correct point assignment and region outcome without a triangle requirement.

## Trial construction and truth

Full calibration uses the frozen 16-query roster. Full development checks the four directed same-frame counterparts in the frozen window against the two reviews of the other, independently labeled distinct window. Also compare the two full development viewpoints in both directions with both opposite-view reviews. The registered overlap in the full development views is a local check; it need not meet the bilateral coverage floor.

Build masked trials from the eight directed calibration moved pairs. For each pair, independently remove the first query-side and last reference-side shared identities in query index order. Make four more trials by trimming a quarter of each cloud, rounded down, using each side's own coordinates. Trim opposite edges for each native axis, in both directions, keeping ties at the retained boundary. Finally, retain three registered identities per side with exactly two in common. Keep both unmasked distinct-region references from the moved roster in all six trials per pair. Crops and deletions simulate unknown visibility. All trials reuse two physical layouts across development and calibration.

Evaluation truth comes from independent wall registration plus reciprocal unambiguous centre agreement within 8 px with equal known hue, following the development landmark distance rule. Pairs require a single eligible partner on both sides. A same-frame pair uses the same frame coordinate system. Masking intersects these truth identities and remaps indices on both sides. Synthetic truth comes from fixture construction. A recovered relation requires one admitted supported hypothesis consisting entirely of expected pairs; exact recovery includes every surviving expected pair. Record compatible wrong-region candidates even when ambiguity blocks a unique result. A sole resolved region that is verified distinct is a false unique region. A unique same-region result with an unverified assignment is tracked separately. Expected eligibility for a positive partial trial is complete input with its registered common identities meeting the frozen count and spatial/triangle rules, computed before invoking either matcher. Below-floor trials are reported separately.

| Preserve full-view evidence | Recover verified 2D geometry in all 6 calibration same-frame and 8 calibration moved directed pairs, and in every eligible development same-frame pair preserved by strict B |
