# Phase 1.6 pilot findings

| Target | Verdict | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NEXT | **INCONCLUSIVE** | Four checkpoints on one map have defensible hidden-target bearings. Missing entrance-origin observations, no independent development support and no evaluation split prevent a benchmark comparison. |
| WP | **INCONCLUSIVE** | Footage identifies the waypoint. A verified automap anchor and discovery history are still missing, before the shared prediction and split gates apply. |

The experiment can recover player marks and some usable geometry from real imagery. In the two earliest frames room outlines are incomplete and there are no confirmed graph edges. At stages 2 and 3, corresponding room outlines and one shared marker can be traced across registered frames. This supports recoverability for some centres and links, with uncertainty about enclosed features, unassigned walls and glyph meaning. Four returns from one layout establish neither reproducibility between reviewers nor predictive performance. [Annotation evidence](PILOT_ANNOTATION_VALIDATION.md)

## What limited the pilot

| Area | Pilot finding |
| --- | --- |
| Data acquisition | The capture and packet pipeline preserves native geometry. The arrival scene identifies the Level 1 stairs. HUD obstruction at stage 3 required surrounding footage to establish NEXT visibility. The waypoint capture lacked an unambiguous correspondence between its scene platform and an automap anchor. |
| Annotation instructions | Entrance axes are allowed without an operational definition or examples. Passage confirmation, endpoint extent and recognition of enclosed features are underspecified. The observed early/late yellow-marker policy shift and stage 2 extent inconsistency warrant a controlled retest. |
| Representation | One confirmed boundary opening can lack a destination centre. That opening cannot justify a dead end in the existing representation unless there is one declared graph link or visible frontier. Wall runs, boundary uncertainty, and centre radii provided by annotators are absent from the predictor signature. Stage 3 reaches seven centres but has only two links. |
| Feature extraction | Player marks are available with stated radii of 3–8 px. Manual centre estimates are 5–20 px, with no repeat-review measurement. The current model can summarize centres and links, but automating the graph would require reliable room segmentation and evidence for links or frontiers. The images suggest wall and marker measurement may be more reproducible, which remains untested. |
| Predictor logic | Both methods accept missing orientation and have an explicit pooled path. The diagnostic has zero independent D2R support and no canonical entrance origin. The empty-table abstentions provide no comparison of the two methods' predictive power. |
| Information availability | These checkpoints provide little confirmed room connectivity early, and exits outside visible geometry may leave multiple completions plausible. The pilot cannot estimate how often different complete layouts share the same observable geometry or whether target direction can be learned. |

Returned annotation times were 1.00, 0.76, 2.50 and 1.05 minutes, totaling 5.31 minutes. These are self-reports for frame annotation alone. Capture, packaging, registration, verification and potential adjudication are separate effort. There are no same-frame repeat annotations from which to estimate disagreement or reproduce the timing.

## Follow-up options

| Option | Expected information gain | Effort and dependencies | Risk and blocker addressed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A · Continue on remaining layouts | More independent layouts could support pooled priors and expose variety in revealed graphs. | High compared with repeating two packets. Requires complete layout labels, deduplication, disjoint partitions, verified anchors and usable entrance-relative marks. | Repeats unresolved conventions across nine clips. Addresses sample size after the input and truth gates are reliable. |
| B · Revise annotation protocol | Tests whether different reviewers can locate the arrival centre or a justified axis, measure visible passage extents and distinguish a one-exit outline from an enclosed structural feature. | Low for a two-packet test. Requires a versioned guide with observable rules and fresh blind reviewers. Keep version 1 returns with their own guide and store any replacements separately. | Moderate if illustrations bias the reviewer or ambiguity is forced into a label. Directly addresses the missing origin and the passage/dead-end inconsistencies. A waypoint proof is a separate Role B capture check. |
| C · Compare raw geometry | Tests repeatability of wall endpoints, opening markers and relative positions before constructing room graphs. | Medium. Requires same-frame repeat marks and a predefined measurement procedure. New representation and benchmark decisions would require follow-up approval. | Appearance and HUD effects could dominate a geometric signature. Addresses confirmed loss of visible geometry and room-segmentation ambiguity. |
| D · Investigate static references | Authorized DS1 presets, DT1 tiles and Automap.txt mappings could test whether the glyphs and enclosed shapes correspond to authored openings or objects. | Medium to high. Requires explicit Phase 2 approval, build-specific asset access and provenance, and independent comparison against observed D2R imagery. | Classic or preset mappings may diverge from this D2R build and cannot supply a verified per-recording WP anchor by themselves. Potentially addresses marker semantics and template identity. [Prior asset research](RESEARCH.md) |

**Smallest next step, pending owner approval.** Run option B on the stage 0 arrival packet and one opening-rich packet, using separate source-only reviews and a versioned guide. Record elapsed time, disagreements, and whether reviewers can mark a common entrance centre within their stated radii, justify an axis or explicitly leave it null, agree on the shared opening's extent, and produce a parser-consistent mapping of dead ends without invented links. One reviewer receives one frame at a time and no later or target evidence. If the centre remains unrecoverable, use that result to decide whether a controlled origin or raw-geometry comparison is warranted. Defer processing the remaining layouts until this identifies a viable observation protocol. WP also needs a Role B capture that connects the labeled in-scene platform to a repeatable automap point before entering scoring.
