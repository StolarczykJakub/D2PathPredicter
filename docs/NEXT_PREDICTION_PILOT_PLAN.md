## Bearing and eligibility

Use native automap `u` right, `v` down and a fixed view scale. Translate player and stair centre into one reference plane using independent wall landmarks. Take the vector `(du, dv) = target − player`. The native angle is clockwise from map up, `degrees(atan2(du, −dv)) mod 360`. The zero vector yields `UNKNOWN`. Reuse the older angle utility at zero radius and apply the four-sector eligibility rule below. The historical eight-sector results remain part of their original benchmark. [Angle utility](../research/geometry_experiment/coordinates.py) · [Coordinate model](ARCHITECTURE.md#inputs-and-coordinates)

The benchmark chooses the dominant signed component in grid `(X,Y)`, with an exact magnitude tie assigned to X. For the documented fixed isometric convention, `du = sx × (dX − dY)` and `dv = sy × (dX + dY)`, with positive scales. The four grid sectors project to native quadrants because `dX² − dY² = (du/sx) × (dv/sy)`. Sector boundaries are map up, right, down and left at 0°, 90°, 180° and 270°. Verify the two diagonal wall families and constant axes for the chosen display setting. If the axis convention is uncertain, the case remains unresolved.

| Grid sector | Native quadrant | Native angular midpoint |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| `−Y` | Right and up | 45° |
| `+X` | Right and down | 135° |
| `+Y` | Left and down | 225° |
| `−X` | Left and up | 315° |

Use a conservative summed radius `R` in native pixels from player and target localization, each independent registration link, and rounding. The existing translation check uses the longest pair of non-target landmarks and an off-axis held-out point. Use at least three anchors per link, log held-out residuals and add the documented 6 px allowance per accepted link. For a multi-link bridge, add all allowances. A truth label qualifies only if `R < min(|du|, |dv|)`. Touching a boundary or including the origin yields unresolved truth even though the nominal four-sector tie rule is defined. Later footage must independently establish both the stair's association to the displayed automap glyph and that this target is still hidden at Stage 0. A scene tooltip proves destination identity. Additional pixel and history evidence establishes the glyph and visibility. [Wall registration utility](../research/marker_fingerprint/independent_packets.py) · [Direction protocol](../benchmarks/PROTOCOL.md#comparison-and-metrics)

Keep outcome codes for missing observation or player, missing or uncertain target anchor, unverified registration, uncertain visibility, already discovered, uncertain bearing and duplicate or disallowed exposure. These determine evaluator eligibility. Reserve predictor `UNKNOWN` for an eligible observation on which a method issues no sector, with a reason for insufficient support, ambiguity, unresolved orientation, unavailable geometry or search truncation.

- **Report on one eligible roster.** Publish total independent layouts, all primary checkpoints and eligible primary counts. Per method and layout, report correct, incorrect or `UNKNOWN`, with reasons, truth sector, issued sector, available votes, and native angular error to the issued sector's midpoint. Report orientation availability, coverage as issued divided by eligible, conditional accuracy as correct divided by issued, and correct predictions per eligible layout. With zero issued predictions, conditional accuracy and angular error are undefined.
- **Compare at matched coverage.** Report risk–coverage counts over the supported ranks without overriding abstentions. Order A and B by emitted vote share and then winning support. Order C by independent supporting layout count. Break ties with the ascending hash of the frozen opaque query ID. Compare at `k = min(issued by both methods)` and show the matched counts. Also show paired wins, losses and shared abstentions by layout on the same eligible roster. A zero common coverage leaves that comparison unevaluable. Include layout-group uncertainty when reporting an interval and retain the small integer denominators. [Benchmark comparison protocol](../benchmarks/PROTOCOL.md#comparison-and-metrics)
- **Exploratory signal.** Propose advancing only if C issues bearings on at least two eligible layouts and at least 60% of the eligible evaluation cohort, beats the strongest evaluable directional baseline by at least one correction at matched coverage of at least two layouts, and has a positive paired net gain in correct predictions across the eligible cohort. Compare against A and against B wherever supported, with B orientation availability explicit. Equivalent or stronger prior performance favors the simpler directional route.
