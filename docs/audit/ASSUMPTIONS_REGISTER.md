# Assumptions register

Status applies to the scope in each row. **Verified** means direct inspection or a reproducible deduction supports the claim. **Partially verified** means a narrower version has evidence. **Unverified** means the decision needs new evidence. **Contradicted** means a source or counterexample conflicts with the claim as phrased. Some claims below are deliberately stronger than Phase 0's wording so the limits are explicit.

| ID | Assumption scoped to the project | Status | Evidence or evidence needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A-01 | Classic Catacombs Level 2 has a waypoint and a next-floor landmark. Normal stairs arrival is a sensible test case. | Verified | Blizzard lists the waypoint and reconstructed classic placement calls cover both landmarks. D2R eligibility is recorded separately. [Area evidence](PHASE_0_AUDIT.md#map-generation-and-observation-audit) |
| A-02 | A pinned classic provider exposes an offline, known-seed generation API with usable room geometry. | Partially verified | The `libd2` 1.14d code and ABI expose it. No pinned build, licensed asset manifest, Windows 10 execution or independent anchor comparison exists locally. [F-03](FINDINGS.md#f-03) · [F-12](FINDINGS.md#f-12) |
| A-03 | Classic and D2R generate equivalent Catacombs Level 2 geometry and landmark placement for the same seed and context. | Unverified | No matched authorized seeds or paired geometry and landmark comparisons. Blizzard reports automap presentation changes. A direct held-out prediction test can assess utility without matched seeds. [F-01](FINDINGS.md#f-01) · [F-04](FINDINGS.md#f-04) |
| A-04 | The first three visible rooms yield useful NEXT and WP bearings on unseen D2R layouts. | Unverified | Neither target has real, target-hidden pilot results. Test the targets independently with matched-coverage baselines. [F-01](FINDINGS.md#f-01) · [F-09](FINDINGS.md#f-09) |
| A-05 | A deterministic generator implies a unique inverse from partial imagery. | Contradicted | Determinism establishes a function from inputs to output. The audit constructed different possible targets under one allowed observation. The D2R collision rate remains unknown. [Identifiability](PHASE_0_AUDIT.md#what-early-imagery-can-identify) |
| A-06 | A unique zero-mismatch reference proves the generating map or a correct bearing. | Contradicted | The closed-pool counterexample returns a unique false fit for both targets. [F-05](FINDINGS.md#f-05) |
| A-07 | An entrance-facing prior is a cheap, sensible baseline. It yields a particular accuracy in D2R. | Partially verified | The classic branch makes an orientation cue plausible and the prior is cheap by construction. D2R visual reliability and predictive performance are unknown. [Map generation](PHASE_0_AUDIT.md#map-generation-and-observation-audit) · [F-04](FINDINGS.md#f-04) |
| A-08 | Shape or orientation of the target's room fixes its global bearing from the entrance or player. | Contradicted | Classic selection depends on eligible room list, RNG and adjacency constraints. The shape describes its connector and the same shape can be translated relative to the player. | A-10 | Collision outlines can be converted into faithful D2R automap geometry with one coordinate transform. | Contradicted | Tile-to-cell selection, marker rules, variants, reveal and cropping are additional operations. A fixed-setting proxy's adequacy remains unverified. [F-04](FINDINGS.md#f-04) |
| A-11 | More revealed geometry materially improves the actual predictor while targets remain hidden. | Unverified | Information cannot hurt an ideal estimator in expectation on a fixed cohort, while observed lift may be negligible or the actual method may degrade.
